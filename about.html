About HOLT NEWS

HOLT NEWS is an independent education channel. Our aim is simple: make English feel usable by connecting it to things people are already talking about. We check each story against published news reports and show our sources on screen.

How our videos are published

We use a small private tool, HOLT NEWS Publisher, to upload our own finished videos to our own accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It runs only on our own computer, is used only by HOLT NEWS, and does not offer sign-in or any service to other people. It uses the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API. YouTube use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
