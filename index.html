Learn easy English from today's news, in about a minute.

HOLT NEWS makes short vertical videos for Korean speakers who want to learn English. Each video tells one real news story in plain Korean, then teaches an English word or sentence that fits it, read aloud by narration.

See recent episodes or follow us on your favorite platform.

What we publish

  • Morning news — three stories every morning: economy, world, and something fun.
  • Single-story shorts — one story and one useful English expression.
  • News words — a word that appeared in the news, with an English example sentence.
  • The HOLT Journal — a research paper explained in everyday language.
