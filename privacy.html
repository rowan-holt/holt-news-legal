Privacy Policy — HOLT NEWS Publisher

Last updated: October 19, 2026

HOLT NEWS Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the HOLT NEWS channels (@rowanholt_official). It is used only to upload the owner's own short videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • OAuth tokens for the owner's YouTube and TikTok accounts, used only to upload videos and read basic channel/creator info (channel name, username, allowed privacy options).
  • The video files and captions the owner chooses to upload.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer in a local file that is not shared or uploaded anywhere. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Third-party users

The app does not collect any data from viewers or other users of YouTube or TikTok.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access) or TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions), and delete the local token file.
